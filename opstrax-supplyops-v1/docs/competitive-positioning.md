# OpsTrax SupplyOps — Competitive Positioning

## High-Level Position

OpsTrax is not a broad ERP clone. It is a narrower, deeper operational platform for controlled facilities and enterprise supply operations where the priorities are auditability, separation of duties, offline resilience, device-driven execution, and restricted-tenant governance.

## Relative Positioning

### SAP Ariba

- **Where Ariba is strong:** large procurement network, supplier collaboration, mature enterprise footprint.
- **Where OpsTrax wins:** tighter fit for controlled facilities, stronger operational execution surfaces, clearer demo-to-deployment path, and less implementation overhead for the core supply workflow.
- **Procure-to-pay angle:** invoice extraction, matching, exception management, and export posture are surfaced in one governed workflow instead of being split across separate finance and AP tools.

### Coupa

- **Where Coupa is strong:** spend management, procurement workflows, enterprise adoption.
- **Where OpsTrax wins:** deeper warehouse and receiving execution, stronger offline/device posture, and a governance model built around operational evidence rather than finance-only spend control.
- **Procure-to-pay angle:** OpsTrax keeps invoice review tightly coupled to receiving, evidence, and audit, which matters in controlled environments.

### Oracle SCM / WMS

- **Where Oracle is strong:** broad suite depth, mature enterprise controls, extensive configuration.
- **Where OpsTrax wins:** faster deployment, less suite sprawl, clearer audit/evidence flow, and a product design centered on controlled-facility operating reality rather than generic enterprise breadth.
- **Procure-to-pay angle:** OpsTrax is narrower but more opinionated about invoice governance, denial logging, and export posture.

## Why OpsTrax Scores Well in RFPs

- audit-first and evidence-aware by design
- tenant isolation is visible and provable
- restricted-tenant behavior is real, not cosmetic
- offline capture and sync are part of the core product story
- device operations are operational, not an afterthought
- AI is bounded and governed, which reduces compliance risk
- the platform is narrower than suite vendors, so it can land faster and with less training burden

## Core Differentiators

1. **Stronger on execution**
   - Warehouse issue, receiving, device trust, offline review, and audit are all part of the same operational chain.

2. **Stronger on governance**
   - Every critical action is audit-logged and permission-checked server-side.

3. **Stronger on controlled-facility fit**
   - Restricted tenants can be intentionally constrained without breaking the product model.

4. **Stronger on demo-to-real transition**
   - RC1 is honest about what is complete, what is sandboxed, and what remains roadmap.

5. **Stronger on bounded AI**
   - AI is not positioned as an autonomous actor. It is read-only in RC1 and subject to backend governance.

6. **Stronger on governed invoice intelligence**
   - RC1 shows extraction confidence, match confidence, exception queues, and export delivery posture without faking a live ERP acknowledgment.
