# Vantelyx CLM Product Blueprint

## Product objective

Build a modern CLM product that can satisfy government/public-sector and enterprise CLM requirements while differentiating through AI-assisted intake, risk intelligence, obligation execution, renewal control, vendor evidence collection, and low-training user experience.

## Core personas

1. Business requester
2. Legal reviewer
3. Procurement owner
4. Finance approver
5. Security/privacy reviewer
6. Vendor/counterparty user
7. Executive sponsor
8. Auditor/read-only reviewer
9. System administrator

## Core modules

### 1. Command Center
Portfolio KPIs, contract value exposure, open obligations, high-risk items, renewal exposure, audit activity, and fast actions.

### 2. AI Intake
Classifies agreement type, detects counterparty, extracts dates/terms/risks, checks playbook deviations, suggests route, and creates contract records.

### 3. Business Request Portal
Structured intake form for non-legal users. Reduces bad email-based requests and incomplete metadata.

### 4. Repository
Searchable system of record with metadata, versions, documents, status, risk, owners, departments, dates, and value.

### 5. Contract Workspace
Contract cockpit with AI summary, risk score, next action, clause intelligence, documents, workflow, and obligations.

### 6. Workflow Studio
Policy-based approval routing by value, risk, clause deviation, vendor type, department, data/privacy trigger, and authority.

### 7. Obligations
Turns contract language into actionable owned tasks with due dates, evidence requirements, status, priority, and audit trail.

### 8. Renewals
Tracks renewal notice windows, auto-renewal risk, owner decisions, notice evidence, spend exposure, and renegotiation tasks.

### 9. Vendors
Counterparty profiles with active contracts, insurance status, data-processing status, performance score, review date, and portal-ready evidence collection.

### 10. Risk & Compliance
Clause playbook, deviations, DPA/security triggers, audit trail, controls, evidence gaps, and policy exceptions.

### 11. Analytics
Cycle time, execution velocity, contract risk mix, value by department, renewal exposure, bottlenecks, and CSV export.

### 12. Templates & Clause Library
Approved templates, versioning, preferred language, fallback positions, escalation rules, and risk categories.

### 13. E-Signature & Execution
Signature packet readiness, signer route, executed document storage, and certificate/audit capture.

### 14. Integrations
SSO, document storage, e-signature, ERP, CRM, AI/OCR, notifications, and BI connectors.

### 15. Admin & Security
RBAC, permissions, audit access, SSO readiness, tenant boundaries, integration governance, and reset/demo controls.

## Differentiators

- Low-training UX: business users guided by request portal and AI intake.
- Obligation-first operations: focus after signature, not only pre-signature.
- Vendor evidence self-service: future portal for certificates and questionnaires.
- Renewal money protection: avoid auto-renewal and missed notice losses.
- AI playbook comparison: not just extraction, but policy-aware review.
- RFP-ready audit posture: prove approvals, evidence, and compliance path.
